About Us

Rwanyangwe High School is committed to providing quality education in a safe, disciplined and supportive learning environment.

🎯 Our Vision

To develop responsible and successful learners who are prepared to make a positive contribution to society.

📖 Our Mission

To produce quality education and self reliant citizens.

⭐ Our Values

Discipline, integrity, respect, hard work and excellence.

Academics

Our academic programmes support students in developing knowledge, skills and confidence for their future.

🔬 Sciences

Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Mathematics.

📚 Humanities

History, Geography, Christian Religious Education and other humanities subjects.

💻 ICT

Computer studies, digital skills and technology.

School Facilities

Rwanyangwe High School provides facilities that support effective learning, practical skills and student development.

💻 Computer Laboratory

Our computer laboratory provides students with practical computer skills, digital literacy and hands-on experience with modern technology.

💧 Water Sources

The school provides reliable water sources to support drinking, sanitation, hygiene and the general wellbeing of students and staff.

🏫 Classrooms

Our classrooms provide a comfortable and conducive environment for teaching, learning and academic development.

📚 Library

Our library provides students with access to books and learning resources that support reading, research and independent study.

🧪 Science Laboratories

Practical learning facilities that support experiments and hands-on learning in Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

⚽ Sports Facilities

Facilities that encourage physical fitness, teamwork, discipline and participation in sporting activities.
